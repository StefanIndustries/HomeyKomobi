Keep an eye on your motorcycle or scooter with your Komobi Moto GPS tracker.

Log in with your Komobi Moto account and add every vehicle in your garage as its own device. Everyone in your home can add the vehicles of their own Komobi account: you log in each time you add vehicles. Each vehicle shows whether it is parked or riding, the theft alarm, the battery of the tracker, the vehicle and the key fob, the odometer, its location and how far it is from home.

Use the flow cards to act when your vehicle starts riding or parks, when the theft alarm goes off or is switched on or off, when it arrives home or leaves, when the vehicle or key fob battery runs low, when you leave your key fob behind, or when Komobi stores a new ride with its distance, duration, speed and driving score.

Add the vehicle status widget to your dashboard to see the status of a vehicle at a glance.

This app is not made by or affiliated with Komobi. It uses the same online service as the Komobi Moto app, so your vehicle needs an active Komobi subscription.
